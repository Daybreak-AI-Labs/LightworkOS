# Reviewed retention

Compliance previews the saved effective periods, selected workspace (or registered
workspace fleet), eligible record counts, dependencies and known preservation
gates. Editing the form invalidates the preview. A review expires after ten
minutes before its first dispatch. Deletion requires the same policy, scope and
recorded holds, current global-administrator authority and an acknowledged audit
record. Each store effect rechecks authority and policy under the installation's
authority fence; hold mutations share that fence.

Every existing privacy-rights case is checked read-only with its integrity and
workspace binding, even when that optional feature is disabled. An active legal
hold or retention restriction blocks broad deletion for the entire workspace:
these stores cannot reliably identify every record belonging to a case subject.
An unreadable or invalid ledger blocks deletion too. CLI/automatic retention uses
the same gate. External holds are not discovered; the records owner must confirm
them separately. The checkbox cannot override a recorded hold.

Durable receipts retain per-store dispatch and outcomes. Completed steps are not
repeated, unknown prior outcomes never become proven zero, and missing audit
acknowledgements remain unresolved. A failed or skipped store does not imply that
no old records existed. Runtime, browser and PostgreSQL acceptance have not
been qualified.
