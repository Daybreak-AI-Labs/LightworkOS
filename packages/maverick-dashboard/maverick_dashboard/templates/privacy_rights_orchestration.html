About this page’s safeguards
  • The person’s email or ID and your ticket number are stored only in a protected, non-reversible form. They are never shown again.
  • Deleting data in another system needs approval from a second administrator first.
  • Closing a request needs approval from someone other than the person who opened it.
  • Connected systems (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce, ServiceNow) are only contacted once your administrator has set them up.
  • A manually recorded result is your statement. A document fingerprint does not independently verify its contents. A completed request does not certify permanent deletion from retained copies or backups.
