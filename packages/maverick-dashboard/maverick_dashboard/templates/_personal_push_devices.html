Your private notification destinations

Connect a private ntfy topic to your account. All devices subscribed to that topic may receive notifications. A separate topic per phone gives you separate controls; Lightwork cannot prove which phone received or read a message.

In your ntfy account, reserve a topic, set access for everyone else to Deny all, and create an access token. A provider plan with topic reservations may be required. Subscribe to that private topic in the ntfy app on the devices you want to notify. Lightwork checks the authenticated account and restriction before sending; changes at the provider remain outside Lightwork's control.

Only you can control this destination in this workspace. Its token and topic are protected in saved storage and never given to Copilot. A new destination starts paused.

Destination quiet hours

These hours follow your selected time zone through daylight-saving changes. They apply to tests too and never have an automatic urgent override. Your personal timing and subscriptions above also apply to automatic task alerts.

Checking privacy contacts ntfy with your token to read the current account reservation. It sends no notification. Only a separately confirmed test or an enabled destination can send a message.
