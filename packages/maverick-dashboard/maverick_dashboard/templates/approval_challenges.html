What the local check does

The practice explores one fictional request and two reviewers, up to 12 steps and 50,000 possible situations. It finds a short failure sequence in a deliberately weakened copy, then tries that sequence against this installation’s approval safeguards. The check uses a fresh disposable local copy, with no customer settings, credentials or network connections and a 10-second deadline.

It checks that the request is unchanged and the reviewer still holds or can take it. It also checks that the review has not expired and that an earlier review cannot be reused after undo. These checks can refuse an outdated sign-off; they cannot grant reviewer permission or reduce the required sign-offs. This selected practice does not establish complete protection, a new invention, business improvement or protection in another product. No live approval rule is installed or activated.
