Storage permissions, protection and limits

Amazon must enable Object Lock and versioning and permit listing versions, reading exact versions and retention, and writing objects with retention. Keys stay in Connections; do not put them in Chat.

This protects the retained version during its lock period. It does not prove that your storage administrators are independent, that records after a checkpoint are correct, or that Amazon will always remain available. For stronger separation, arrange storage access and custody with a separate administrator.

A check reads all retained versions in the configured folder, including versions hidden by a deletion marker. It refuses incomplete coverage beyond 10,000 versions, 64 MB of checkpoint content or 60 seconds. Each copy holds at most 2,000 checkpoints and 4 MB.
