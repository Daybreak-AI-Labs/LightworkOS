About this page’s safeguards
  • Visitors whose browser asks not to be tracked always get the original version. So do uses that must always be on, and layouts that fail accessibility or equal-button checks.
  • An experiment compares 2 to 10 versions and can be checked at most 20 times; visitor records are kept at most 90 days.
  • A statistical winner is not a legal or accessibility approval. Review the result before changing your published banner.
