What an import preserves

Instructions can require AI interpretation and connected tools. The complete visual translation review identifies approximations and steps needing repair. Imported drafts do not inherit the source platform's approvals or credentials. Existing drafts stay unchanged unless you explicitly select their replacement. Schedules and starts from other services stay off.
