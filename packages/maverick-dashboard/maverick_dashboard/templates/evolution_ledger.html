Keep a history checkpoint

Save a small reference file somewhere you trust outside this installation. Later, choose that same original file to check whether the earlier stored history still matches. Newer records can be added without breaking the check.

This covers the complete native record of learning adoption, restoration, and their related recording steps. It contains dates, counts, and fingerprints, with no learned content. It does not cover every platform action or confirm who approved a change.

Checking whether a complete checkpoint is available.

The comparison depends on the original file you kept independently. A newly generated or edited file cannot establish what was recorded before. This is not external custody or proof that every real activity was recorded.

Supported history and limits

Only the permanent native learning history is supported, up to 4,096 stored records and 16 MB of source history. Older-format files and temporary histories cannot be checkpointed. The entire supported history must pass its stored checks; no partial download is offered. If history is unavailable, refresh before relying on it.

Check a receipt

Choose a downloaded receipt to check its contents and look for a matching change in this workspace's current history. A match does not prove who approved it or establish independent custody of the whole history. No private approval material is accepted here.
