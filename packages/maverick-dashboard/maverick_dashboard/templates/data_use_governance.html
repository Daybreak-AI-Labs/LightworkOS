About this page’s safeguards

When no rule applies, the answer is always “block”. Each change records who made it, and your name is added by Lightwork, not typed. A rule or reason recorded by one person must be switched on or used by a different person. Database rules are prepared from the saved records only; nobody can type database commands here.
