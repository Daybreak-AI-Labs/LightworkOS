About this page's safeguards
  • Only evidence a person has approved can support a suggestion.
  • Partial, missing, conflicting or low-confidence evidence is never offered as an answer that can be added.
  • Approving picks exact questions; it does not change the assessment.
  • Before adding answers Lightwork checks that the evidence and the assessment are unchanged. It fills only unanswered questions and never overwrites an answer a person gave.
  • Suggestions are not legal conclusions and do not prove a supplier's claims.
