Employee policy

Employees are not enrolled until you enable their companion. Access stays limited to tasks they own in this workspace.

AI conversation sends the employee's prompts, eligible saved history and any permitted task titles and status to your configured AI provider. It uses your configured writing model and may incur provider charges. No tools or external actions are enabled. These history controls do not change the provider's retention policy.

Saving a disabled companion or “Do not save” deletes its saved conversation history from the active store. “Do not save” keeps new messages only in the employee's open page. Shortening retention can remove older history. Activity metadata and separately managed backups are not removed by this setting.

Choose an employee to review their policy.

Administration without conversation access

This page shows policies and activity records. It does not provide access to employee companion messages. Content access would require a separate, explicit and logged process; that process is not available in this preview.

No connected documents, external actions, learned memory or live voice are enabled here. AI replies are drafts for the employee to review and copy. Write actions are blocked, so there are no write approvals to configure.
