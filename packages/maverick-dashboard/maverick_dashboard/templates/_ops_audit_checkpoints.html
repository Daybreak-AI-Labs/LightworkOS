History checkpoints

A checkpoint is a signed note of how long one day of the log was and how it ended, kept in a separate folder. Checking it can detect changes to its retained prefix. Later entries after the checkpoint are outside that commitment. Keep its receipt outside this installation to check whether the checkpoint history was replaced or rolled back.
