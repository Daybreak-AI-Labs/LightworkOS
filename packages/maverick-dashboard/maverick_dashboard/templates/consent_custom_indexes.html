About this page’s safeguards
  • Searches match answers exactly; nothing is guessed about anyone.
  • Search values are protected while they are processed and are never saved.
  • If answers changed since the last build, searching stops until the question is rebuilt, so results are never out of date.
  • Results list the people who gave an answer; they are not proof of identity or of consent for anything else.
