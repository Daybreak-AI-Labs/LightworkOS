Turning a subscription off stops sends that have not yet passed their final check. An attempt already sent cannot be undone. Changing its address stops queued messages to the previous address.

Messages use a subscription's current signing secret when one is saved. The installation's configured address list uses the installation signing secret. Without the applicable secret, a message is unsigned. Check the receiving system's requirements before turning a subscription on.
