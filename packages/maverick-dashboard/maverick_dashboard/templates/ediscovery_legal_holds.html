Legal holds

A legal hold keeps records for a legal matter. While a hold is active, bulk deletion of old records is paused for this workspace. The people named as custodians cannot erase their conversations, tasks and attachments that fall within the matter’s record types and dates. Everything else stays deletable. Only auditors and administrators see matter details.
