What a receipt proves

A verifier checks the recorded decision for consistency. It cannot prove that the underlying action really occurred. A separately retained checkpoint is needed to detect later changes to committed history.

  • Signed
    The recorded row verifies against the issuer's key.
  • Chained
    The row is linked to a tip the issuer committed.
  • Anchored
    That commitment matches a checkpoint supplied independently of this receipt.

Keys held on this computer, including keys supplied by an external service, remain available to its signing process. External key sourcing alone cannot prevent it from re-signing history.
