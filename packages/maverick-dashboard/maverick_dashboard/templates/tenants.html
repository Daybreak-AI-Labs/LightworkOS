Why use workspaces?
  • Its own records. Work done for one workspace can't be seen from the others.
  • Its own documents. Uploaded files and customized agents stay in the workspace that owns them.
  • Its own budget. A daily spending limit and usage records for each workspace, so costs roll up cleanly for billing.
  • Its own access. People can have a different role in each workspace.
