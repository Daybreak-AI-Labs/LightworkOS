Try your safeguards

Test a prompt, answer or action against your safeguards before it goes out.

What this does and doesn't prove

The text you enter is checked against your safeguards, your hidden-information filters and your AI use rules for the chosen system, then thrown away. A result here does not prove that other tools or live systems apply the same safeguards.

Technical details

Evaluate a prompt, model output, or proposed action against local Shield, secret/PII redaction, and the active operational-policy authority. The exact asset revision and digest are checked against Model Risk, and policy facts come from its current approved declaration—not from this form. Raw content is evaluated in memory and is not persisted by this plane. A local result is not proof of third-party runtime coverage or production acceptance.
