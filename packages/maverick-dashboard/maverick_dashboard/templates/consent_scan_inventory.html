About this page’s safeguards

Only “Scan now” and “Run due scans now” visit your websites; listing and reviewing never do. A result counts as complete only when every page loaded without problems, and even then a scan cannot prove a tracker is absent. The person who records a review is taken from their sign-in; changing the tracker list needs a second, different administrator to approve. Scanning pages behind a sign-in is not available yet.
