# NDA playbook (sample)

Standard positions for mutual NDAs signed by Northwind Fixtures:

1. Mutual obligations: both parties protect each other's information.
2. Term of confidentiality: 3 years from disclosure (2 to 5 years acceptable).
3. Governing law: England and Wales, or the counterparty's home jurisdiction
   if it is in the EU or the United States.
4. No non-solicitation, non-compete or exclusivity clauses in an NDA.
5. No assignment of intellectual property; an NDA grants no licence.
6. Residual knowledge clauses are not accepted.
7. Standard carve-outs: public information, already known, independently
   developed, required by law.

Anything outside these positions is a deviation for counsel to review.
