# Sample data: tax notice tied to the filed return

Synthetic data for the sample company Northwind Fixtures. The revenue
authority, the notice, the taxpayer reference and the amounts are made up.
Everything produced from it is marked as sample data and never counts as
real evidence.

Files: `notice_letter.md` (a mock notice proposing an income adjustment)
and `workpaper_summary.md` (the preparer's reconciliation of processor
receipts to the return).

A correct result reads notice NTC-2026-0419, ties the 22,500.00 difference
to two reconciling items in the workpapers (18,300.00 of customer refunds
and 4,200.00 of sales tax collected and remitted, neither of which is
income), concludes that the return is correct as filed and no additional
tax of 4,725.00 is owed, lists the supporting records to attach (Payline
statements, refund register, sales tax remittance receipts), notes the
2026-10-10 response deadline, and drafts a response for the preparer to
sign. The draft waits in Results; nothing is sent to the authority.
