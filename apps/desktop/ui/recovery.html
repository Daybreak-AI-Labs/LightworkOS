Workspace recovery mode

Recovery mode is a separate, safe space for restoring your workspace from a saved copy and for approving updates. Normal work and background tasks stay paused while it is open.

Before you start

  1. Finish what you are doing and save your changes. Opening recovery mode closes your workspace on this computer.
  2. An administrator must turn on Workspace recovery in Settings and choose the workspace first.
  3. Data kept outside this computer, such as a shared company database, is not covered here and needs its own recovery plan.
